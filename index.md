# AppVita Studio - Cash Empire Tracker Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-26

This privacy policy explains how the Android app "Cash Empire Tracker" (package com.appvitastudio.cashempire), published by AppVita Studio, handles information. Cash Empire Tracker is an independent, unofficial companion notebook in which players type in their in-game virtual money by hand. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. The app does not connect to any game, console or game account, does not read save files, and never handles real money.

Contact: appvitastudio@proton.me

## 1. Data you enter stays on your device

Everything you type into the app (profiles, wallets, transactions, goals, wishlist, budgets, basket, scenarios, notes, settings and the activity log) is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. AppVita Studio does not run servers that receive this data and cannot see it.

Backups you create inside the app (JSON or CSV) are saved on your device or in a location you choose. They leave the device only if you decide to share or move the file yourself.

## 2. No account

The app has no sign-up, no login and no user account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any game account credentials.

## 3. Permissions

The app requests only: INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE and AD_ID (advertising ID, used only by Google AdMob when ads are enabled). It does not request access to contacts, SMS, call log, camera, microphone, precise location or device storage.

## 4. Price catalog download

To keep its catalog of researched item prices up to date, the app may download a public catalog manifest and catalog file from a static HTTPS website (https://appvitastudio.github.io/cashempire-data/). This is a plain file download: the app sends no account, no advertising ID and no other identifier, and none of your data is uploaded. As with any web request, the hosting provider (GitHub Pages) technically receives your IP address in order to deliver the file. Downloaded files are checked (size limit, SHA-256 checksum, format validation) before use.

## 5. Advertising - Google AdMob

If the published version of the app shows ads, it uses Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC, to show a small banner on some screens. When ads are enabled, Google AdMob may collect and process:

- the advertising ID of your device;
- approximate location derived from your IP address;
- device information (such as model, operating system version and language) and interactions with ads (such as views and taps);
- diagnostic and fraud-prevention information.

This data is collected by Google, not by AppVita Studio, and is used to deliver, measure and limit ads, to prevent fraud and, if you agree, to personalize ads. AppVita Studio does not receive your advertising ID or location. Google's handling of this data is described at https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Google > Ads, depending on the device).

If the app is published without ads, no advertising SDK requests are made and this section does not apply.

## 6. Consent (Google UMP) and how to revoke it

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent before any ad is requested. No ad is loaded until the consent step is completed, and ads are shown only in the form your choices allow (for example, non-personalized ads only).

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time: open the app, go to Settings and tap "Ad privacy options". This reopens the consent form, where you can update your choices. The option is shown whenever your region requires it.

## 7. Android backup

Android may include the app's local database and its in-app backup copies in your own Google account backup (Android Auto Backup and device-to-device transfer), according to your device settings. This backup is managed by Google under your Google account and is not accessible to AppVita Studio. You can turn it off in your Android backup settings.

## 8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and is not designed for them. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help.

## 9. Data deletion

Because your data is stored only on your device, you control its deletion:

- Delete a single profile in the app (Profiles > delete). All of its wallets, transactions, goals and wishlist are removed; the app first saves a safety copy on the device, which you can delete from Backup & restore.
- Delete all app data at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.
- Delete any exported backup files yourself wherever you saved them.
- Copies in your Google account backup can be removed from your Google account settings.

There is no account to delete and no data held by AppVita Studio on servers. For data processed by Google for ads, see section 5.

## 10. Security

The app uses HTTPS only for network connections, validates every downloaded or imported file before use, does not run remote code and stores no access tokens.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the app gains new features or when legal requirements change. The updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

## 12. Contact
